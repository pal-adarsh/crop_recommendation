Viva Finding: Why AdaBoost Scores ~31%

Standard AdaBoost boosts single-threshold decision stumps sequentially. On a 22-class problem with subtle continuous feature overlap (e.g. mungbean, blackgram, and lentil share near-identical N-P-K profiles), one-split hyperplanes cannot construct the complex non-linear decision boundaries required. This is a theoretically expected finding demonstrating why ensemble bagging (Random Forest) outperforms sequential boosting of weak stumps on high-cardinality agricultural classification.
