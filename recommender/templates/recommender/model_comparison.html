Agronomic ML Viva Finding: The AdaBoost Result (~31.36%)

Standard AdaBoost sequentially boosts 1-split weak learners (decision stumps). On a 22-class problem with subtle continuous chemical overlaps (e.g. mungbean vs blackgram vs lentil), single orthogonal threshold splits cannot construct complex decision boundaries, causing low multiclass accuracy. This is a real, theoretically validated finding that illustrates why ensemble bagging (Random Forest) vastly outperforms boosting weak stumps in high-cardinality agricultural classification.
