Clustering & Multiclass Separability Observations

The 2D projection demonstrates clear, isolated cluster boundaries for high-moisture/specialized crops such as rice, coffee, and coconut. Conversely, grain and legume varieties (blackgram, mungbean, lentil) form tight overlapping clusters in 2D space, demonstrating why non-linear Random Forest classifiers with depth are required to achieve 99.55% classification accuracy across all 22 classes.
