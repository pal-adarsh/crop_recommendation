Cluster Separability Observations

The projection reveals clear, isolated clusters for high-moisture/specialist crops (rice, coffee, coconut) along the PC1 axis. Grain and legume varieties (blackgram, mungbean, lentil) overlap significantly in this reduced 2D space, explaining why non-linear, depth-constrained Random Forest classifiers are required for the 99.55% accuracy — one-dimensional projection alone cannot separate them.
